How this list is made

  • A script reads Apple's public App Store charts for ten markets: India, United States, United Kingdom, UAE, Singapore, Australia, Canada, Germany, Japan and China.
  • It reads each app's version history for update notes that mention iPhone Duo or its folding screen.
  • "Duo support added" is the release date of the earliest version whose notes mention iPhone Duo. "Latest update" is the date of the app's newest version, which may have nothing to do with iPhone Duo.
  • Choosing a market shows every listed app that is on that market's App Store, with that market's rating and store link. "Popular" sorts by the number of ratings there, and "Top rated" by the rating.
  • With no market chosen, ratings and links are from the App Store in India (or the app's home market if it is not sold in India). "Needs iOS" is the oldest iOS version the app runs on.

Good to know

  • Visitors can suggest an app with "Suggest an app". The daily refresh checks the app's update notes and adds it only if they say it supports iPhone Duo.
  • Every app shows how it was confirmed. A green dot means its own update notes say so. An amber dot means it was reported as optimised by Apple or the press; those apps carry the date of the report in place of the date support was added, and a link to the report.
  • Other apps that support iPhone Duo without saying so are not listed, so the real number is higher.
  • A few notes mention iPhone Duo for a bug fix and not for new support. Tap an app to read its note and check.
  • Apple's "Made for iPhone Duo" badge and the SDK version an app was built with are not in the public data, so they are not shown.
  • The list is built automatically, so it can miss apps or include one by mistake. Check an app's App Store page before relying on it.
