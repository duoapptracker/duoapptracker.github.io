How this list is made

  • Several times a day a script reads Apple's public App Store charts for ten markets (India, United States, United Kingdom, UAE, Singapore, Australia, Canada, Germany, Japan and China) and searches the App Store for apps that mention iPhone Duo.
  • It reads each app's version history and keeps apps whose update notes mention iPhone Duo or its folding screen. A "Duo" in the app's own name does not count.
  • Apps that support iPhone Duo without saying so in their notes are added from Apple's iPhone Duo collections, press reports, the community (X, Reddit, YouTube and similar), or after the site owner downloads and tests them on an iPhone Duo.
  • Choosing a market shows every listed app that is on that market's App Store, with that market's rating and store link. "Most popular" sorts by the number of ratings there, and "Top rated" by the rating.
  • With no market chosen, ratings and links are from the App Store in India (or the app's home market if it is not sold in India). "Needs iOS" is the oldest iOS version the app runs on.

Good to know

  • Every app shows how it was confirmed. A green dot means its own update notes say so; its date is the release date of the earliest version that mentions iPhone Duo.
  • An amber dot means it was reported as optimised (by Apple, the press, the community or the site owner) but its notes do not say so. Its date is the earlier of the report date and the app's last update when it was added, and it does not change later.
  • If an app's own notes later mention iPhone Duo, it turns green and takes the real date.
  • Visitors can report a missing app with "App missing?". The next refresh checks the app's update notes and adds it only if they say it supports iPhone Duo.
  • Some apps have more than one source (for example their own notes and a report), so the Confirmed by numbers can add up to more than the total.
  • A few notes mention iPhone Duo for a bug fix and not for new support. Tap an app to read its note and check.
  • Apple's "Made for iPhone Duo" badge and the SDK version an app was built with are not in the public data, so they are not shown.
  • The list is built mostly automatically, so it can miss apps or include one by mistake. Check an app's App Store page before relying on it.
