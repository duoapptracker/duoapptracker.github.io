How this list is made

  • A script reads Apple's public App Store charts for nine markets: India, United States, United Kingdom, UAE, Singapore, Australia, Canada, Germany and Japan.
  • It keeps the apps that are available on the India store and reads each app's version history for update notes that mention iPhone Duo or its folding screen.
  • "Duo support added" is the release date of the earliest version whose notes mention iPhone Duo. "Latest update" is the date of the app's newest version, which may have nothing to do with iPhone Duo.
  • "Popular in" filters by the markets whose top charts list the app right now.
  • Ratings, prices and links are from the App Store in India. "Needs iOS" is the oldest iOS version the app runs on.

Good to know

  • Apps that support iPhone Duo without ever saying so in their update notes are not listed, so the real number is higher.
  • A few notes mention iPhone Duo for a bug fix and not for new support. Tap an app to read its note and check.
  • Apple's "Made for iPhone Duo" badge and the SDK version an app was built with are not in the public data, so they are not shown.
  • The list is built automatically, so it can miss apps or include one by mistake. Check an app's App Store page before relying on it.
